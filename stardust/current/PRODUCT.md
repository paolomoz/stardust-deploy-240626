# PRODUCT — Pebl (hellopebl.com)

Register: brand (marketing landing).

## Product Purpose
Pebl is an AI-powered Employer of Record (EOR) and global HR platform. It lets companies hire, pay, and support talent across 185+ countries without setting up local entities, with built-in compliance guidance.

## Users
Founders, HR leaders, finance and legal teams at companies expanding globally; talent-acquisition teams hiring across borders.

## Brand Personality
Confident, credible, lightly playful ("Build teams anywhere. Yes, even there."). Editorial and premium via a display serif (FK Roman Standard) paired with a distinctive body face (Sharp Earth). Trustworthy: leans on G2 #1 compliance rating and a logo wall of recognizable enterprises (Honeywell, Yelp, CAA, Penguin Random House).

## Anti-references
Generic white SaaS templates; busy multi-CTA hero collages; flat all-cards layouts.

## Design Principles
Let the serif voice and the teal/mint palette carry the brand; lead with credibility (numbers, logos); keep hierarchy clear.
