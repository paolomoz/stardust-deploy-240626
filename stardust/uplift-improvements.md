# Improvements — https://hellopebl.com/

1. **[dated-pattern]** Centered hero with double CTA over a busy multi-photo collage reads as a 2021 SaaS template — the "Build teams anywhere" headline (FK Roman 85px) is strong but competes with a crowded supporting illustration instead of being given room to breathe.
2. **[ia-clutter]** The page runs ~9,600px tall with 8+ near-equal-weight sections (End-to-End Global HR, Meet Pebl appears twice, G2, 24-hour hire, competition, insights, FAQs). The duplicate "Meet Pebl" block and the flat hierarchy give a board-level reader no clear path: every section shouts at the same volume.
3. **[contrast-or-density]** Generous-to-a-fault vertical rhythm — multiple sections at ~690px tall with thin content force heavy scrolling; the trust strip ("Trusted by global leaders": Honeywell, Yelp, CAA, Penguin Random House) is buried mid-page at 903px instead of anchoring credibility immediately under the hero.
4. **[cliché]** Stat presentation is timid — "185+ countries" and "24 hours" are the brand's hardest credibility claims but appear inline in body copy rather than as a dedicated, confident metric band. A board audience reads numbers first.
5. **[missed-opportunity]** The brand owns a genuinely distinctive type system (FK Roman Standard display serif + Sharp Earth) and a confident teal/mint palette (#04404e arctic, #6bdacd pacific), yet the layout deploys them at template scale — the editorial-serif voice and the marquee logo wall are the most ownable assets and are under-amplified.
